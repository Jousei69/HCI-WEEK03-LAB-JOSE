Welcome to Japan - Where Tradition Meets Innovation

Japan is an island country in East Asia, located in the northwest Pacific Ocean. It is bordered by the Sea of Japan to the west and extends from the Sea of Okhotsk in the north to the East China Sea and Taiwan in the south.

Top Destinations

Japan offers a unique blend of ancient traditions and cutting-edge modernity, from historic temples in Kyoto to the neon-lit streets of Tokyo.

Must-Visit Places:

  • Tokyo: Vibrant capital city with diverse neighborhoods
  • Kyoto: Ancient capital with thousands of temples
  • Osaka: Known for its modern architecture and food scene
  • Hokkaido: Northern island famous for skiing and nature
  • Hiroshima: Peace memorial and nearby Miyajima Island

Rich Cultural Heritage

Japan's culture combines influences from Asia, Europe, and North America. Traditional arts include tea ceremonies, calligraphy, and flower arranging.

From the graceful movements of geisha performances to the powerful strikes of sumo wrestling, Japanese culture offers a fascinating glimpse into both ancient traditions and contemporary life.

Quick Travel Tips

  • Best time to visit: Spring (March-May) or Autumn (September-November)
  • Visa-free entry for many countries (up to 90 days)
  • Japanese Yen (JPY) is the local currency
  • Japanese is the official language
  • Remove shoes when entering traditional accommodations and some restaurants
